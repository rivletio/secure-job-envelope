Encrypted exchange

Two desks mint post-quantum identities, publish a signed directory and a one-time prekey bundle, then seal a traveler so only the recipient can open it — forward-secret by default. Real ML-KEM-1024 and ML-DSA-87, running entirely in your browser.

ML-KEM-1024 · HKDF-SHA-384 · AES-256-GCM

Nothing leaves your browser. The transit artifact is a .sje envelope — ciphertext — so the traveler id, buyer and part number never appear in the clear. A one-byte flip, the wrong keystore, a forged envelope and a silent downgrade are all refused.
