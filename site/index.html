Secure Job Envelope

When a buyer orders custom parts, two things are easy to get wrong: the price has to match the exact spec, and the details shouldn't leak on the way to the shop. Secure Job Envelope is a shared record for that job — a price is always pinned to one exact spec, and what's sent is encrypted. The demos below run the real thing in your browser: read the plain-language walkthrough, then poke at it yourself. New here? Start with Lifecycle.

The whole workbench →

The complete app: build a job, bind and award quotes, create a passphrase-protected post-quantum identity, and seal or open an encrypted .sje — all in your browser, nothing sent anywhere.

Everything you see is computed locally with the same modules shipped in the library, the Rust CLI and the MCP server. The demos never transmit a traveler; the “encrypted exchange” seals and opens entirely within this one tab.
