Lifecycle & binding

Compose → quote → amend → re-quote → award, running the real reference library in your browser. A quote binds to the exact revision it priced; amend the spec and it goes stale; the award locks the traveler at L2.

Nothing leaves your browser. traveler_hash is a SHA-384 over the canonical quoteable body — change material or quantity and every prior quote stops matching.
